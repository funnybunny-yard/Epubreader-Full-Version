## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

**EPUBReader** is a powerful Firefox add-on designed for reading eBooks directly in your web browser. With the **latest version**, you can enjoy a seamless reading experience without the need for a separate eBook reader. This full-featured solution allows you to manage your eBook library efficiently while customizing your reading environment to your preferences. 

Download EPUBReader today to access your eBooks with ease and take full advantage of your library right from your Firefox browser!
